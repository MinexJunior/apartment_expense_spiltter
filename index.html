Welcome to Condo-Calc! How to get started:

In Ethiopian urban centers like Addis Ababa, sharing residential blocks means splitting fluctuating service costs. Condo-Calc automates this task and gamifies payments with neighbors. Follow these 4 easy steps:

Step 1: Setup a Room 🏘️ Create a custom room directory or join an existing virtual block (e.g. Block B-4).
Step 2: Add Block Residents 👥 Add households, flat numbers, occupants, and custom exemptions (such as Ground Floor water pump discounts).
Step 3: Distribute Core Bills 💰 Distribute core condominium bills: **Water**, **Electricity**, and **Security** amongst members instantly.
Step 4: Pay & Win Streaks 🟩 Mark your bills as paid, win early payment discounts, and grow your **Coffee-Bean Streak Board**!
